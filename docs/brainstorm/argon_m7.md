### 1. Model Choice for Speech + Background Music (GTX 1650 4 GB)
- **`htdemucs` (default)**: [FEASIBILITY.md](file:///F:/sound_system/docs/FEASIBILITY.md#L25-L26) measured **2.84 s GPU / 638 MB VRAM** (25.2 s CPU) on 6 s audio. Its hybrid time+spectrogram U-Net best preserves speech plosives and formants without spectral gating holes.
- **`Kim_Vocal_2.onnx` (secondary option)**: Measured **4.41 s GPU / 2312 MB VRAM** (15.0 s CPU). Gives drier inter-word music suppression and is **1.7× faster on CPU** than `htdemucs`, making it a good "Tighter music removal" option, though near the 3 GB VRAM ceiling on GPU.
- **MelBand / BS-Roformer ([models.json](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/models.json#L38-L66))**: **Reject for 4 GB Turing (fp32, no Tensor Cores).** Checkpoints are ~250–450 MB; multi-band attention over 8 s chunks (`overlap=8` in [mdxc_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/architectures/mdxc_separator.py#L229-L263)) exceeds 3–4 GB VRAM, is ~8–15× slower, and calls `sys.exit(1)` on load errors ([mdxc_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/architectures/mdxc_separator.py#L108-L112)).

### 2. Instrumental / Karaoke Stem Construction
- **Demucs (`drums + bass + other` vs. `mix − vocals`)**: Summing `drums + bass + other` in float32 gives stronger vocal rejection than `mix − vocals` (which leaves phase-residual "ghost talk" where vocal phase/level is slightly under-predicted).
- **MDX (`mix − vocals`)**: Keep `invert_using_spec=False` ([mdx_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/architectures/mdx_separator.py#L177-L183)). **Critical bug**: `invert_using_spec=True` returns a C-contiguous `(N, 2)` array ([spec_utils.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/uvr_lib_v5/spec_utils.py#L575-L580)), which triggers a bug in [common_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/common_separator.py#L320-L334) (`write_audio_soundfile` casts float32 to `int16` = **all zeros** and writes a `2N` mono file when `F_CONTIGUOUS` is False).
- **Dedicated instrumental model**: Unnecessary extra download for v1; Demucs 3-stem sum + MDX residual suffice.

### 3. Chunking Long Files & 44.1 kHz Round Trip
- **Windowing**: Demucs (`segment=7`) and MDX (`segment_size=256`) already chunk GPU inference internally ([apply.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/uvr_lib_v5/demucs/apply.py#L216-L251), [mdx_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/architectures/mdx_separator.py#L344-L389)); only **CPU RAM** scales with duration (~105 MB/min Demucs, ~63 MB/min MDX) and Demucs reloads `.th` weights per `separate()` call ([demucs_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/architectures/demucs_separator.py#L110-L121)). Use **5–10 min windows** with **2–5 s raised-cosine crossfades** via [chunks.py](file:///F:/sound_system/backend/media/chunks.py#L50-L109).
- **Per-window gain & silence hazards**:
  - Pass `normalization_threshold=1.0, amplification_threshold=0.0` to `Separator` — the default `0.9` peak-normalizes each window independently ([mdx_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/architectures/mdx_separator.py#L155-L170)), causing gain jumps at crossfades.
  - **Silent-window bypass**: All-zero windows raise `ValueError` in [common_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/common_separator.py#L217-L220) and divide by `ref.std() == 0` (`NaN`) in [demucs_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/architectures/demucs_separator.py#L170-L171).
- **48k ↔ 44.1k round trip**: Pre-resample windows `48000 → 44100` with `soxr` (`VHQ`) so `librosa.load(..., sr=44100)` is a no-op, then `44100 → 48000` with `soxr` and pad/trim to the exact window frame count (`48000/44100 = 160/147` can otherwise drift by ±1–2 samples).

### 4. Issues & Gaps in the Draft Plan ([2026-11-15-m7-separation.md](file:///F:/sound_system/docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-15-m7-separation.md))
- **Broken MDX `segment_size=128` OOM step**: [mdx_separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/architectures/mdx_separator.py#L115-L133) only uses ONNX Runtime when `segment_size == dim_t` (`256`); `128` converts the model via `onnx2torch` onto PyTorch CUDA. On MDX GPU OOM, fall back **directly to CPU** (keeping `segment_size=256`).
- **Extra outputs lifetime (`output="both"`)**: [runner.py](file:///F:/sound_system/backend/pipeline/runner.py#L219-L257) appends `result.extra_outputs` as-is and deletes `work/` at job end. Extra stems must be encoded via `encode_audio` into `job_root/output/` (expose `out_dir`/`audio_format`/`stem` on [OpContext](file:///F:/sound_system/backend/operations/base.py#L27-L41)).
- **Offline & download hooks**: `Separator.load_model()` fetches `download_checks.json`, `vr_model_data.json`, and `mdx_model_data.json` ([separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/separator.py#L463-L663)); [capabilities.py](file:///F:/sound_system/backend/core/capabilities.py#L64-L72) must verify those JSONs exist for offline readiness. Hook `Separator.download_file_if_not_exists` ([separator.py](file:///F:/sound_system/.venv/Lib/site-packages/audio_separator/separator/separator.py#L350-L373)) to emit `model_downloading{model_id, pct}`.
- **Pipeline order & conflicts**: Order `30` (before `declick` 38, `denoise` 40, `dereverb` 42, `enhance`/breaths 50, `silence` 60) is optimal so music doesn't corrupt VAD masks, noise-floor estimates, or room-resonance detection. However, [rules.py](file:///F:/sound_system/backend/operations/rules.py#L14-L18) (`KARAOKE_DISABLED_REASONS`) must also disable `declick` and `dereverb` when `output="instrumental"`.

### 5. Objective Tests Without Real Music Recordings
- **Realistic fixture**: Extend [make_fixtures.py](file:///F:/sound_system/scripts/make_fixtures.py#L86-L102) with harmonic sawtooth/triangle chords (changing every 2 s, LP 3.5 kHz), bassline, and drum transients mixed with `speech.wav` (including a 1 s music-only intro/gap) and with `tests/fixtures/real/*.m4a` at −10 dB SMR (`pytest.skip` if missing).
- **Metrics**:
  - **$\Delta\text{SI-SDR}$** against ground-truth stems: $\ge +8\text{ dB}$ on `vocals`, $\ge +6\text{ dB}$ on `instrumental`.
  - **Music-only intro/gap RMS**: reduced $\ge 15\text{ dB}$ in `vocals`; Whisper transcript retains `"quick"`, `"brown"`, `"fox"`.
  - **Karaoke**: Silero VAD speech ratio $< 10\%$ of input and 300–3000 Hz vocal-band energy reduced $\ge 12\text{ dB}$.
